## Introduction

* This datasheet documents my individual engineering work for the Automatic Pet Food Dispenser project in EGR 304. It provides an overview of the system design, my contributions to the electronic design, and links to the supporting engineering documentation.

### Project Summary

* The Automatic Pet Food Dispenser is designed to automate pet food dispensing through electronic timing, sensing, and control. The system uses the PIC18F57Q43 Curiosity Nano microcontroller and supporting electronic components to provide timing, optical sensor monitoring, signal conditioning, and control functions. My work focuses on the Optical Sensor Monitoring and Fault Detection subsystem and selecting components that meet the design's electrical and power requirements.
* For additional information about the overall project, see the [team report](https://embedded-systems-design.github.io/EGR304TeamTemplate/).

### My Contribution

* My contributions focus on component selection, electronic circuit design, microcontroller interfacing, and engineering documentation. I have selected the TCST1103 optical slot sensor, MCP6004 operational amplifier, and L7805 voltage regulator for the subsystem design. My related laboratory work includes PIC18F57Q43 programming, ADC, UART, and PWM functionality, along with KiCad schematic and PCB design, custom symbols and footprints, design-rule checking, and Gerber file preparation.
