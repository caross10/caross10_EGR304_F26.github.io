## Overview

This schematic is designed for the optical sensor monitoring and fault detection subsystem of the Automatic Pet Food Dispenser. It includes the TCST1103 optical sensor, MCP6004 operational amplifier, PIC18F57Q43 Curiosity Nano board, L7805 voltage regulator, and supporting resistors and capacitors. The optical sensor detects movement of the dispensing mechanism, and its signal is routed through the signal-conditioning circuit to the microcontroller for monitoring and fault detection. The schematic also includes a push button, indicator LEDs, power supply circuitry, and a connector for interfacing with the other subsystems.
